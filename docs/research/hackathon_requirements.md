# Build with Gemini XPRIZE Requirements Notes

Source checks performed June 13, 2026.

## Key Dates

- Submission period: May 19, 2026 at 10:00 AM Pacific Time through August 17, 2026 at 1:00 PM Pacific Time.
- Judging period: August 18, 2026 through September 15, 2026.
- Finalist pitch and winners announced: on or around September 25, 2026.

## Primary Judging Signals

- Business viability.
- AI-native operations.
- Category impact.

## Submission Evidence To Prepare

- GitHub repository shared with `testing@devpost.com` and `judging@hacker.fund`.
- Three-minute video showing AI live in production and executing key decisions.
- Written narrative of 500-1000 words explaining day-to-day AI usage, human versus AI responsibilities, jobs and economic opportunities created or enabled, and the build story.
- Revenue evidence, such as Stripe dashboard export, bank statement, invoices, or P&L.
- Expense disclosure, including marketing and customer acquisition spend even if zero.
- Product evidence, including agent execution logs, API usage records, and dashboard screenshots.
- Customer evidence, including real customer contact info and feedback/testimonials where available.
